OUR STORY

18 Years of Precision Motorcycle Engineering

Founded in 2008 by passionate club racers and certified motorcycle mechanics.

Founded in 2008 by passionate club racers and motorcycle mechanics, our workshop began with a single lift and a simple promise: honest diagnostics, precision race-grade torque standards, and zero upselling. What started as one mechanic's vision has grown into a state-of-the-art 12-lift facility serving thousands of riders across the Midwest.

Over the past 18 years, we've invested in digital Dynojet dynamometers, dealership-grade motorcycle scan tools, and laser alignment systems, building a team of certified master technicians who ride every day.

Today, our workshop is one of the most trusted independent motorcycle service and tuning centers in the region, combining craftsmanship with modern diagnostic technology to keep your machine in peak track and road condition.

Our Mission

To provide transparent, precision motorcycle service that empowers every rider to experience peak performance and absolute safety. We deliver honest diagnostics, factory-spec repairs, and upfront pricing — every time, for every rider.

Our Vision

To be the premier independent motorcycle tuning and service center in the country — recognized for our unwavering dedication to rider safety, mechanical craftsmanship, and an unmatched garage experience.
